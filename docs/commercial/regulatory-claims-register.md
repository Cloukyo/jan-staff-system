# Regulatory claims register

Last reviewed: 4 September 2026

This register records the evidence and limits behind competitive-readiness product behaviour. It is not legal advice and must be reviewed when authoritative guidance changes.

| Topic | Supported factual claim | Product behaviour | Limits and review trigger | Authoritative source |

## Prohibited claims

- Do not describe the product as certifying EYFS, Ofsted, DBS, employment-law or payroll compliance.
- Do not state that employee or volunteer classification alone determines DBS eligibility.
- Do not state that the product calculates statutory holiday pay, tax, PAYE, National Insurance, pensions or payslips.
- Do not claim the evidence export replaces source documents or management judgement.
